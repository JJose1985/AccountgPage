Accounting process in five practical steps

A simple reference page that outlines how accounting data moves from source documents to financial reporting.

1. Collect source documents

Gather invoices, receipts, payroll records, bank statements, and any other supporting evidence for business activity.

2. Record transactions

Enter each transaction accurately in the journal or accounting system with the correct date, amount, and account classification.

3. Post to the ledger

Move journal entries into the general ledger so balances can be tracked for assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, and expenses.

4. Review and reconcile

Reconcile bank accounts, inspect unusual balances, and correct any posting errors before month-end reporting.

5. Prepare reports

Use the verified ledger balances to produce the trial balance, income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow summary.
